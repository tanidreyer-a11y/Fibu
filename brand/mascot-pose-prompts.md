# Fibu robot: extra poses (Google Flow, images)

Fibu already has **wave** and **stand** cut-outs in the app. These five fill the moments the
app needs: celebrating a workout or PR, flexing on a streak, thinking while the AI works,
resting on rest days, and an "oops" face for errors.

## How to run it in Flow

1. Flow → **Images** (Nano Banana). Ratio **3:4**.
2. Attach the reference: `Downloads/Robot_standing_in_confident_pose_2K_20260915143714.jpeg`
   (the plain grey-background one). This keeps the robot identical.
3. Paste the **Character lock** below, then ONE pose prompt under it. Generate 4, keep the best.
4. Save the winners anywhere (Downloads is fine) and tell me the file names. I cut them out and
   wire them into the app.

Note: unlike the Hustlers film look, these should look like a clean studio render, straight-on
or slight three-quarter, because they get cut out and sit on app screens.

## Character lock (paste first, every time)

```
CHARACTER LOCK: Use the attached image as the exact character, the FIBU robot. Keep its exact
design: oversized round helmet head on a small body, matte oxblood-maroon armour panels with
black joints and black hands, a glossy black visor face showing white segmented LED eyes and an
LED smile, and the glowing red "FB" monogram on the left chest. Do not change its proportions,
colours or parts, and do not add new parts, text or logos.
STAGE: full body with the whole robot in frame and clear margin on every side, centred, on a
plain seamless light-grey studio backdrop, with only a soft contact shadow under the feet. Soft
even studio key light from the front-left, a gentle rim light, the same clean product-render
look as the reference. 3:4 portrait.
NO: props unless named, floor lines, background objects, text, extra logos, cropped hands or
feet, motion blur, extra fingers.
```

## 1. Celebrate (workout finished, new PR)

```
The robot jumps for joy: both arms thrown straight up in a wide V with fingers spread, one knee
lifted, feet just off the ground so the soft shadow sits a little below them. Its visor shows
happy closed upturned-arc eyes and a wide open LED smile. Camera slightly low, three-quarter
front view.
```

## 2. Flex (streak milestone, level up)

```
The robot does a proud double-biceps flex: both arms raised, elbows out at shoulder height,
fists clenched beside its helmet, chest pushed out, feet planted wide. Its visor shows
confident half-closed eyes and a lopsided grin. Straight-on view, camera at chest height.
```

## 3. Think (AI is working, planning your program)

```
The robot stands thinking: one hand raised to the bottom of its helmet like stroking a chin,
the other arm folded across its waist, helmet tilted slightly to one side. Its visor shows one
LED eye raised higher than the other and a small flat sideways smile. Three-quarter front view.
```

## 4. Rest (rest days, recovery)

```
The robot sits on the floor cross-legged, leaning back slightly with one hand on the floor
behind it, the other hand holding a plain red sports water bottle resting on its knee. Its visor
shows relaxed sleepy half-moon eyes and a soft small smile. Three-quarter front view, camera at
the robot's head height.
```

## 5. Oops (errors, no connection, nothing found)

```
The robot looks sheepish: one hand rubbing the back of its helmet, the other hand open at its
side, shoulders slightly raised, helmet tilted down a little. Its visor shows small wavering
LED eyes and a wobbly zig-zag LED mouth. Straight-on view, camera at chest height.
```

## Checking a result

Keep it only if: the FB chest logo is still on the left chest and red, there are no extra
fingers, the visor face is LED (not a painted face), the colour is the same oxblood maroon, and
hands and feet are fully in frame. If one thing is off, re-roll changing only that line.
